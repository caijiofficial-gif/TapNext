How to set up your card

You're all set once you publish. Your TapNext One Tap card is then ready to share your profile with other people.

  1. 1

    Tap your card

    Tap your NFC card on your compatible smartphone to open the TapNext website.

  2. 2

    Activate

    Select Activate Card.

  3. 3

    Sign in

    Sign in with your linked Google account or email, then enter the TapNext Activation Code included in your parcel.

  4. 4

    Build your profile

    Choose your profile theme and add your photos, details, and social media links.

  5. 5

    Preview

    Preview your profile.

  6. 6

    Publish

    Publish your digital profile.
